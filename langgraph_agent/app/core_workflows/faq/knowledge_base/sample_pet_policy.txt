Pet Policy

Section 1: General Rules
Tenants are permitted to keep up to two domestic pets per unit, subject to prior written
approval from property management. Approved pets include cats, dogs under 30kg, and
caged birds. Exotic animals, reptiles, and dogs classified as aggressive breeds under
local ordinance are not permitted under any circumstances.

Section 2: Pet Deposit
A refundable pet deposit of PKR 15,000 per pet is required at move-in, in addition to
the standard security deposit. This deposit is returned within 14 days of move-out,
less any documented damage caused by the pet.

Section 3: Registration
All pets must be registered with the property office within 7 days of move-in or
acquisition. Registration requires proof of current vaccination records. Unregistered
pets discovered during routine inspection will result in a formal notice and a 30-day
window to complete registration or remove the animal from the premises.

Section 4: Tenant Responsibilities
Pet owners are responsible for immediate cleanup of pet waste in common areas, keeping
pets leashed or contained in shared spaces, and ensuring pets do not create excessive
noise disturbances. Repeated verified complaints may result in review of continued
pet privileges.

Move-Out Policy

Section 1: Notice Period
Tenants must provide a minimum of 60 days written notice before their intended
move-out date. Notice given with less than 60 days remaining on the lease term
is subject to a short-notice fee equivalent to one month's rent.

Section 2: Move-Out Inspection
A joint move-out inspection is scheduled within 3 business days of the tenant's
vacate date. The tenant is invited to attend. Photos are taken to document unit
condition against the move-in inspection report.

Section 3: Deposit Return
Security deposits are returned within 14 business days of the move-out inspection,
less any deductions for damage beyond normal wear and tear, unpaid rent, or
outstanding utility balances. An itemized deduction statement is provided with
any partial return.
